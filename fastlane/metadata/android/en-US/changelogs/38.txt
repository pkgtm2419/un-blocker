v2.7.2 (versionCode 38) — System integrations and automation

New in this release:
• Quick Settings tile: toggle or pause protection from your notification shade
• Home-screen widget: live status, start/stop toggle, and 5-minute pause button
• Static launcher shortcuts: Home, Logs, Learn, and Exceptions
• Opt-in broadcast automation with token authentication (Tasker-compatible)
• Hide from recent apps toggle (Privacy & data settings)
• Per-app split-tunnel exclusions — choose apps whose DNS traffic is not filtered
• Logs: long-press multi-select for batch Allow / Block / Reset; inline Quick Rule actions in domain detail
• Decision explainer ("Why?"): every blocked or allowed row now shows the matched rule and source list
• Export v2: CSV with rule attribution columns; JSON export; settings backup and restore
• Exceptions screen: list, search, test, and remove your allow / block exceptions
• Modular searchable Settings with 9 focused sections
• Jetpack Navigation Compose with validated unblocker:// deep links
• Android 16 target SDK 36, Compose BOM 2026.09.00, Room 2.8.5, Kotlin 2.4.21

Bug fixes and hardening:
• Isolated per-app Learn recording — other apps are excluded from the tunnel during a session
• Learn review defaults: nothing pre-selected; ad-domain Allow requires explicit confirmation
• Auto-restart protection after app updates (MY_PACKAGE_REPLACED)
• Honest UPSTREAM_FAIL status: upstream DNS timeouts recorded separately from allowed traffic
